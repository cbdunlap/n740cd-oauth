Privacy Policy

N740CD Office TV Photos accesses Google Drive using read-only permissions for the purpose of retrieving photographs selected by the user for display on a privately controlled Raspberry Pi.

Google Drive data is used only to locate and download image files from the designated "Office TV Photos" folder. Those files may be stored locally on the user's Raspberry Pi for display.

The application does not sell Google user data, share Google user data with third parties, use Google user data for advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to displaying the selected photographs.

The application does not modify or delete files in Google Drive.

The user may revoke the application's access to Google Drive at any time through Google Account permissions.
